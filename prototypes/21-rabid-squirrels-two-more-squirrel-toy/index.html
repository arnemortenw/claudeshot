    How to play

    • ⏱️When it's your turn you have 30 seconds. Everyone else watches. And heckles.
    • 👣← → walks, Space jumps, up to 150 steps a turn. After you fire you get 5 seconds to run, or End turn.
    • 🎯Drag back like a slingshot to aim. Let go to fire.
    • 🔓A round is one turn for every player. Each new round unlocks a new weapon for everyone.
    • 🌊Damage hurts. Water and lava kill. The last player standing wins.
